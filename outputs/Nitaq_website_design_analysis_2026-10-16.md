# Nitaq Academy website design and structure analysis

**Review date:** 16 October 2026. **Scope:** live English and Arabic pages, responsive browser captures, current React/Vite source, and all 18 pages of the October 2026 agency review. This is a design and functionality assessment, not a verified traffic, conversion, Google Business Profile, licence, or PageSpeed audit. No production changes were made.

## A. Existing website: section-by-section audit

The site has two visual systems. The homepage uses its own `Navbar`, `Footer`, `home.css`, Plus Jakarta Sans/Cairo, cinematic green hero, image accordion and bespoke buttons. Other routes use a shared `Header` and `Footer`, with Poppins/Inter and several course templates. This explains why individual pages often feel less cohesive than the homepage. At desktop width the homepage hero is a two-column grid; at 390px it stacks content above the image. The fixed header changes to a drawer at 1100px. The course catalogues use separate card grids. Sampled live routes had no horizontal overflow at 390, 768 or 1024px, but that does not prove every route is clear.

## C. Visual system proposal

**Current source colors:** homepage green `#15803D`, ink `#0F2D1E`, muted `#2D3F35`, paper `#F7F9F4`, border `#DCE5DA`; hero gradient `#122E1C` / `#174F2B` / `#124124`; navbar `#144626`; global template green `#2E7D32`, teal `#2BBBAD`, dark text `#1A1A1A`, light background `#F9FBF9`, dark slate `#0F172A`. Current type: homepage Plus Jakarta Sans and Cairo for RTL; internal Poppins headings and Inter body. The site also uses assorted inline styles and monospace labels. The headline is ~40–60px desktop / 30–42px mobile; some hero/supporting/caption text falls to 13.5/9/8px. Desktop section padding is often 112px; container max width is 1320px. Rounded corners range from 12px buttons to 120px hero-photo corner.

**Suggested tokens:** warm white `#F7F9F4` or `#FAF9F6` (validate against actual logo image), white `#FFFFFF`, ink `#0F2D1E`, body `#344054`, border `#DCE5DA`, brand green `#15803D` with deep green `#144626`, teal `#2BBBAD`, proposed campus-inspired orange `#D96B2B` (sample/approve exact brand value from original art before implementation). On off-white, a green or orange solid button can stand out; on deep green, warm-white is the strongest primary button. Use orange for one high-intent CTA per view only where contrast passes WCAG; not every action. Use teal sparingly in tags/progress rather than replacing green. Reserve dark-green blocks for hero/final CTA and a compact footer band. Alternate warm white and white elsewhere, with a single soft green trust/why block.

**Typography and spacing:** Choose one Latin family across homepage and course templates (Plus Jakarta Sans is already used on home); keep Cairo for Arabic with its own line-height and weight rules. Body 16–18px/1.55–1.7, labels 13–14px minimum where essential, H1 clamp ~36–58px, H2 ~28–42px, readable mobile paragraphs at 16px. Reserve 8–11px only for nonessential metadata. Use a 1200–1320px container, 24px mobile side padding, 64–96px desktop and 48–72px mobile section spacing, 20–28px card gutters, 12–16px button radius and one consistent 16–20px card radius. Prefer static composition and mild hover response to heavy animation. Keep CTA touch targets >=44px and visible keyboard focus.

## D. Proposed homepage wireframe and reusable parts

1. **Header** — logo, Home (logo), SAT, School Tuition, Languages & Exams, Professional Courses, About, Articles (possibly a secondary “Resources” item), language switch, “Book assessment”/Call CTA. Reuse header components with one shared data model.
2. **Hero** — one outcome-led line such as “Clearer progress for your next exam or school year,” followed immediately by SAT/tuition/language specificity. Primary: “Start free SAT diagnostic” to `/sat/diagnostic`; secondary: “Explore programs” to #programs or “Talk to admissions.” Reuse hero image and responsive variants; contrast fix.
3. **Verified trust bar** — licence, current Google rating/count if verified, class formats if confirmed. No fabricated batch size.
4. **Three core program cards** — SAT, tuition and languages with direct destination links. Use existing card assets and URLs. Professional courses get a slimmer separate callout just below.
5. **Why Nitaq / how learning works** — condense current four benefits and five steps to a concise process with factual specifics.
6. **Tutors** — two to four real profiles after consent/credentials; until then, do not show placeholders or anonymous “expert” headshots.
7. **Results + reviews** — verified outcome examples if available, otherwise prioritize authentic reviews and state no numeric outcome. Reuse current review component after UI-language/accessibility review.
8. **Campus / visit** — reuse About photography and location component.
9. **Institutional logo strip** — preserve approved artwork in a simple horizontal row with accurate captions; no new partner-page detour.
10. **Final CTA** — choose SAT diagnostic, school assessment or course enquiry/WhatsApp by intent; do not claim instant booking if form only creates a lead.
11. **Footer** — consistent bilingual navigation, NAP, social and legal links.

The PDF’s seven-step wireframe omits the site’s useful campus/process/partner material. These can stay, compressed; they are not reasons to restart the design.

## E. Navigation architecture

**Desktop visible:** logo/Home · SAT Preparation · School Tuition · Languages & Test Preparation · Professional Courses · About Us · Resources (if space) · language switch · admissions CTA. Contact can be the CTA destination and remain in footer. The SAT link should go directly to `/sat-preparation-sharjah`; tuition to `/academic-excellence`; languages to `/language-trainings`; professional to the existing `/courses` or `/professional-certifications` after confirming which is the actual broad hub. Avoid a mega-menu for all courses at top level. A modest dropdown for Tuition (by board then subject), Languages (conversation and exam preparation) and Professionals (finance/tax, tech/AI, business/HR) is sufficient; full catalog remains an explicit “View all” link. Preserve all old URL targets and internal links in hubs, cards and footer.

**Mobile:** logo + language + Menu. Drawer order mirrors the desktop top level; each item has a direct hub link and a separate expand button, with clear current-route indication and a persistent, non-obscuring admissions action. Close on selection/Escape, manage focus and lock background scroll. Arabic drawer reverses layout and uses Arabic accessible names. No nested four-column menu on a phone.

## H. Risks and guardrails

The greatest risk is publishing unsupported trust signals: licence status, affiliations, trainer credentials, Google review metrics, batch sizes, fee dates and student score claims each need an owner and dated source. The agency’s market figures and scorecard should not become site copy. A second risk is changing architecture while silently dropping crawl paths; retain current slugs, redirects only if unavoidable, breadcrumbs, article links, canonical/metadata/structured-data rules, XML sitemap and reviewed hreflang. The prerender script writes both `dist/sitemap.xml` and `public/sitemap.xml`, so implementation checks must account for generated-file changes. Arabic routes currently use a published-route allowlist; confirm their content before expanding indexation. The fixed mobile header, page auto-scroll, drawer focus and delayed third-party chat need interaction testing. Finally, swapping stock imagery for campus photos should preserve image dimensions, WebP delivery, alt text and mobile crop.
